FIRM RADAR - client monitoring

This is your client app. It is completely separate from your podcast Narrative Radar:
its own folder, its own Start button, its own address.

  Firm Radar (this folder)     http://localhost:8865   clients, crowd checks, weekly briefs
  Podcast Narrative Radar      http://localhost:8765   your show's topics, viral, videos, breaking

The only things you need in this folder:

  Start Firm Radar        Double-click to start. Leave the black window open.
  Open Firm Radar         Opens the client page (http://localhost:8865).
  Install 24-7            One time: makes the Firm Radar start every time you log in.
  Remove 24-7             Undoes that.

The screens (tabs along the top):

  COMMAND        Each client's operations room. The threat radar puts the client at the center;
                 every topic is a blip, and the closer it is to the center, the more urgent.
                 Color = crowd check, size = how much is being said, pulsing ring = spike.
                 Next to it: the threat meter (0-100, and which topic drives it), the crowd
                 check list, the live feed, the 48-hour pulse, and what to watch next.
  OVERVIEW       The original Radar: one card per topic with every number. Add topics here.
  NETWORK        Who is connected: accounts wired to the same message or the same link.
                 A loose cloud is normal; a tight star of new accounts is worth checking.
  SPREAD MAP     Where a story showed up (newsrooms, social, forums, video), in what order,
                 with a replay.
  CASE BOARD, ALERTS, REPORTS, SOURCES   Same as before. REPORTS makes the weekly brief.
  CLIENT VIEW    (button, top right) A clean, light screen to share on a call. No account
                 names, notes or evidence links.

  + New Client   Sets up a private Radar for one client from their name, issues and competitors.

Folders:

  clients     One folder per client: client info, watchlist, reports, and saved data.
              To stop watching a client, move their folder out of here.
  engine      The program. Don't delete it.
